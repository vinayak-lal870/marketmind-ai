# MarketMind AI
### Discover What Your Business Is Missing.
**AI-Powered Business Growth Intelligence Infrastructure — Built for Google Startup Buildathon India 2026**

MarketMind AI is an autonomous business growth intelligence engine designed for SMBs and D2C brands. Unlike reactive AI chatbots that wait for prompts, MarketMind AI builds a persistent **Business DNA** profile, scans market and competitor signals, simulates alternative business futures, and converts high-conviction opportunities into measurable growth experiments.

---

## Core Intelligence Engines (MVP)

1. **Business DNA Engine:** Persistent semantic business profile (products, pricing, target segments, constraints) indexed in PostgreSQL (`pgvector`).
2. **Market Radar & Competitor Blind-Spot Engine:** Surfaces underserved customer segments and competitor weaknesses—strictly separating verified facts, sourced evidence, and labeled AI hypotheses.
3. **What-If Business Simulator:** Models alternative business scenarios (pricing shifts, new customer segments, geographic expansion) across revenue assumptions, operational friction, and risk.
4. **Growth Experiment Studio:** Converts selected opportunities into structured, low-budget validation experiments with clear hypotheses, execution steps, and KPIs.
5. **Outcome Ledger:** Compares predicted vs. actual experiment performance to build a proprietary feedback loop.

---

## System Architecture (Google AI & Google Cloud Stack)

* **Frontend:** Next.js 15, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
* **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, LangGraph orchestration
* **AI & Embeddings:** Google Gemini API (Structured JSON Outputs & Long-Context Reasoning) + Google Text Embeddings
* **Database:** Cloud SQL for PostgreSQL + `pgvector` (strict multi-tenant isolation)
* **Infrastructure:** Google Cloud Run, Cloud Storage, Cloud Logging
